# Marin Montagut reference notes

Source reviewed: https://www.marinmontagut.com/en?utm_source=chatgpt.com

Observed public structure: a small announcement/store-location bar; header with utility links and language/search/account/cart controls; product categories grouped into visual mega-menu families; a central boutique hero with a single CTA; a "Wonders" style category showcase; multiple curated collection/product-grid sections; editorial brand/story pages; and store/address content.

Observed category examples: Porcelain, Glassware, Decoration, Stationery, Textile, Personalization, Fragrance, and New. The Queen St BB implementation uses its own categories and content instead: Tiramisu, Gelato & Sorbet, Space, Objects, Cake Booking, Gift Cards, Wholesale, and Franchise.

Usage boundary: use only high-level information architecture and retail interaction patterns as inspiration. Do not copy Marin Montagut's exact visual design, text, logo, illustrations, product imagery, or distinctive branded assets.
